Our Collection

The Vision

We're rather picky about the vintage furniture that we source. We aim to only offer quality pieces that we would love to live with ourselves. We hold a small but very carefully selected range of investment vintage pieces that are highly collectible. They do sell quickly, so there are always exciting new things arriving.

At Midcenturist SA we are all about restoring and documenting vintage treasures. We don't just 'buy and sell'. We meticulously clean, lightly restore and refinish all our pieces to preserve the integrity of the piece to the best of our ability — back as close as possible to its former glory to last another lifetime.

A Sustainable Approach

Why Buy
Vintage
Furniture?

Buying pre-loved furniture extends its product life. Most of our pieces are more than 40 years old and will last at least another 40+. Vintage furniture is beautiful, timeless, unique, valuable, well designed, high quality, full of character and a future investment. When you buy vintage furniture you buy into a piece of history.

01
Sustainable Living

Reduce, reuse and repurpose — vintage is the most sustainable furniture choice available. These pieces have already endured decades of use and will continue to serve for years to come with minimal maintenance.

02
Investment Value

Each vintage item carries its own unique charm and story — making it more than furniture. A carefully curated piece is a future investment that only appreciates in value over time.

03
Lovingly Restored

Every piece is meticulously cleaned, lightly restored and refinished to preserve its integrity — back as close as possible to its former glory. We care for each piece as if it were our own.
